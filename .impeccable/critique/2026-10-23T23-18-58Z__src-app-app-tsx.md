## Design Specificity Verdict

- **What's specific:**
  - the lander-first 4-step flow, driven by one data list in advisorSteps.ts;
  - the honesty labels;
  - the coordinate convention, rendered straight from code;
  - the refusal to fake Moon imagery.

  No unrelated dashboard could reuse that.
- **What isn't:** the look could ship on any dark admin tool: system fonts, a grey ladder, pills, dashed wells.
- **Atmosphere and colour barely show:**
  - The one "cinematic" element, the viewport gradient, is mostly covered by an 80%-opaque card, and will be effectively invisible on a projector.

## Overall Impression

The restructure worked on its two goals:

- **The lander is now the product's starting point:** step 1 is in the top bar.
- **The screen now says what the product is:** a statement that says it's in development, a factual sources line, and the non-endorsement line.

Both P1 issues from the first critique are resolved. But the honesty that defines the product has become noise: 12 pills, 7 of them "Needs data". And the flow the brief chose as the focal point is the smallest text on screen, at 11px uppercase grey. The biggest opportunity now is hierarchy: say each absence once, and make the 4 steps the largest thing a judge sees.

## What's Working

1. **One source of truth for the steps** (advisorSteps.ts). Headings, the summary and the empty states all render from the same objects, so the honesty copy can't drift between regions. It also leaves room for 0 to N sites later.
2. **Accessible step naming.**
   - StepLabel speaks "Step N" as a single text node and hides the visible numeral.
   - The lander select is announced as "Step 1 Lander", with its reason as the description.
   - The DOM order (header → main → rail → Reasoning) now follows the steps. This fixes the first critique's reading-order issue.
3. **Status doesn't depend on colour, and nothing is made up.** The Unavailable badge's dot is a hollow ring, coordinates are a real monospaced readout, and there are no fake numbers anywhere.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Too many status badges and repeated copy**

- **What:**
  - 12 badges, 7 of them "Needs data", 10 of them grey.
  - The top bar says "absent" four times within about 400px: the badge, "No sourced landers yet", "Lander constraints not sourced yet." and "No sourced data yet".
  - "No verified lunar basemap yet" appears twice word for word (viewport and rail).
- **Why it matters:** judges stop seeing the badges. The signature honesty device becomes wallpaper, and the product reads like an apology screen.
- **Fix:**
  - State each step's status once: in the summary, or in the step's own region, not both.
  - In the top bar, keep the select and one reason, and drop either the step badge or the global badge.
  - Delete the duplicated basemap sentence.
  - Use one phrasing for "comes with the map".
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

**[P1] The focal flow doesn't stand out, and won't read on a projector**

- **What:**
  - The flow is set at 11px uppercase grey, identical to every rail label. The summary heading is the same size as the h1.
  - From 1024px to about 1219px, the centre column is too narrow (about 430px) for the 34rem container query, so the flow stacks vertically. A 1024×768 projector gets the weakest version.
- **Why it matters:** the brief's success test is that a judge can describe the 4-step flow within 10 seconds.
- **Fix:**
  - Give the flow its own size step: numerals at title size, step titles at body-small in sentence case.
  - Make the summary heading clearly larger than the product name.
  - Let the 4-column flow appear earlier, for example by narrowing the side panels between 1024 and 1279px.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable typeset, then /impeccable layout

**[P1, inferred from the CSS; check in a browser] The summary may be clipped on short desktop screens**

- **What:**
  - On desktop the viewport is a grid row with a minimum height of 0 and overflow-y: auto, and the summary card is centred with plain align-items: center.
  - When the card is taller than its row, it overflows equally above and below. The top part paints over the search strip and can't be scrolled back into view.
  - The estimate is about 524px of content against about 484px of space, in a 1024×768 window with browser toolbars showing.
- **Why it matters:** venue projectors and small laptops are exactly where the demo runs, and the first things cut would be the Placeholder badge and the heading.
- **Fix:** use align-items: safe center, or margin-block: auto on the summary card.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

**[P2] The step 3 and step 4 labels don't match their actual state**

- **What:**
  - **Step 4:** it has no badge and says "Select a ranked site…". Next to three flagged steps it reads as working and waiting for input, but nothing can ever be selected.
  - **Step 3:** it says "Needs data", but its real blocker is an undecided method.
- **Why it matters:** these are the only two places where a label and the true state disagree. The brief's principle is "label certainty honestly".
- **Fix:**
  - Give step 4 a blocked reason, for example "Depends on steps 2–3".
  - Pick a label for "method undecided".
  - Both are decisions the brief made, so they need the user's confirmation.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

**[P2] The Placeholder badge is misleading, and the map tools come first**

- **What:**
  - "Placeholder" sits directly above "A lander-first landing-site advisor". It actually means "no map here yet", but it reads as describing the advisor.
  - The not-built search strip comes before the product explanation, both on screen and in reading order. On phones it's the first thing after the top bar.
- **Fix:**
  - Attach the badge to the map line instead, for example "Map · Placeholder".
  - Hide the tools strip while the empty state is showing, since search arrives with the map anyway.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

## Minor Observations

- **Tall top bar:** about 6rem on desktop, because the lander block stacks label, select and caption.
- **Two lander claims:** "No sourced landers yet" and "Lander constraints not sourced yet" sit on adjacent lines and say slightly different things.
- **Wrapping step titles:** at the 34rem threshold each flow column is about 136px, so "2 RANKED SITES" may wrap. Check in a browser.
- **No connecting marks:** the flow track has no nodes or arrows; the order comes from the numerals alone.
- **Empty gap in the rail:** map layers settle to the foot of the rail, which leaves a large empty area in the middle on a 1080p screen.
- **Flow split on narrow screens:** below 1024px, the map layers come between step 3 and step 4.
- **Dim disabled select:** the browser's default styling may dim "No sourced landers yet". Check in a browser.
- **Hard-to-read subtitle:** about 49 uppercase, letter-spaced characters, and it wraps on phones.
- **Duplicated panel name:** the Reasoning panel's label repeats its heading. aria-labelledby would keep the two in sync.
- **Overstated description:** outside this surface, the index.html meta description ("Interactive browser for CLPS lunar landers…") claims more than exists.
