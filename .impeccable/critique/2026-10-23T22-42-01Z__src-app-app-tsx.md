## Design Specificity Verdict

**Design review (A):** honest and well made in its details, but interchangeable in its structure, and it points towards the wrong product.

- **The structure is generic:** a top bar with search, a left rail of layers and filters, a map in the middle and a details panel on the right. That's the standard GIS template.
- **The accent isn't unique:** #6ea8fe is (to the reviewer's knowledge) Bootstrap 5.3's dark-mode primary tint, on a generic blue-slate neutral ladder. The lunar names in DESIGN.md don't change the values.
- **The character is in the details:**
  - the status badge whose dot is a hollow ring for Unavailable;
  - dashed strokes meaning "absent";
  - the coordinate-convention readout, with signed bounds, a true minus sign and monospaced digits;
  - a viewport that refuses to fake terrain.
- **The architecture contradicts PRODUCT.md:** "the lander is the starting point, not the map", yet the lander appears nowhere on screen. The shell is set up as a generic catalogue of places, which PRODUCT.md explicitly says this product is not.

## Overall Impression

The shell is a trustworthy foundation: semantic, flat, honest, and with no invented data anywhere. But it answers "what does a lunar map tool look like?" rather than "what does a lander advisor look like?" A judge's first five seconds are a small title, a big black field and the word PLACEHOLDER. It reads as honest, but empty. The single biggest opportunity is to make the lander-first, explained-score flow visible in the structure, even while every step is still disabled.

## What's Working

1. **The status badge system** (DataStatusBadge.tsx, .module.css:20-44).
   - The text carries the meaning; the dot's shape (filled, or hollow for Unavailable) backs it up, so status never depends on colour alone.
   - Every disabled control is linked to its reason with aria-describedby.
   - It's PRODUCT.md's "label certainty honestly" principle made visible.
2. **The convention panel renders from one source of truth.** COORDINATE_CONVENTION (selenographic.ts:27-37) feeds both the display and the validation, so the text can't drift from the logic. With signed bounds and tabular monospaced digits it reads like instrument output. It's the most product-specific element on screen.
3. **The structure is sound.**
   - The 1px grid gap draws the hairlines automatically.
   - minmax(0,1fr) and min-height: 0 stop overflow.
   - Each side panel scrolls on its own inside a fixed full-height shell.
   - The landmarks are clean: a header, two labelled asides and a labelled main.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The lander, the product's main object, is missing, and the layout is map-first.**

- **What:** the sidebar is Layers, plus Filters for Missions and Candidate sites (ExplorationSidebar.tsx:23-76). The prime top-bar slot is a coordinate search, a map-first, Google Maps-like control (TopBar.tsx:26-42).
- **Why it matters:** judges can't see what makes the product different, and the team will rehearse, and keep building on, the wrong structure.
- **Fix:**
  - Reorder the sidebar into the advisor's steps:
    - **1 Lander:** a disabled selector, badged "Needs sourced lander constraints".
    - **2 Ranked sites.**
    - **3 Time windows:** badged as having an undecided method.
    - **4 Reasoning.**
  - Move Layers to a secondary position.
  - Consider giving the top-bar slot to the lander selector rather than the search.
  - Show no factor names until docs/LANDER_RULES.md defines them.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable shape

**[P1] The screen never says what the product is, or where its data comes from.**

- **What:** the subtitle is the generic "Lunar exploration workspace", and APP_TAGLINE isn't used. The viewport message only announces an absence. There's no mention of the 16 researched sources or of the non-endorsement commitment.
- **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md says judges need "where its data comes from and why sites rank". Today they get neither.
- **Fix:** turn the viewport label into a short product statement and keep the Placeholder badge:
  - the lander, ranked sites and explained scores flow;
  - a factual status line such as "16 sources researched · 0 adopted", pointing to docs/DATA_SOURCES.md;
  - one line saying NASA doesn't endorse the project.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable onboard

**[P2] The honesty copy is the smallest, faintest text on screen.**

- **What:** every reason, note and helper line is 11px (--font-size-xs) in Dim Ash. Badges are also 11px, and nothing on screen is larger than 18px.
- **Why it matters:** the product's main trust signal is the hardest text to read in a live demo. Viewing from across a room is an assumption, since PRODUCT.md only says it's "shown live".
- **Fix:** move notes up to --font-size-sm, keep Dim Ash only for truly secondary text, and consider a slightly larger base size for demo legibility.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable typeset

**[P2] The status labels are inconsistent, and will clash with the labels planned for ranking.**

- **What:**
  - One status appears under three labels.
  - The Basemap row, the clearest "absent" item, has no badge.
  - "Not built" and "no sourced data" share a single badge.
  - The badge's status list (DataStatus, DataStatusBadge.tsx:5) has no room for Verified / Assumption / Illustrative. Once those arrive, "Provisional" and "Assumption" will sit side by side with overlapping meanings.
- **Fix:** use one label per reason ("Not built" vs "Needs sourced data") and give the Basemap row a badge. Decide now how the data-status labels and the ranking-input labels will coexist.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

**[P2] The disabled search takes over the top bar.**

- **What:** the label, the input and a help line stack in a column up to 24rem wide. Review A estimates that the help line wraps to two lines, making the bar about 110–120px tall, for a control that does nothing. That height is an estimate; it hasn't been seen in a browser.
- **Fix:** cut the help text to one line, or fold it into a badge beside the label. Alternatively, make it work: the parser and formatter already exist and are tested, so the search could show a validated, formatted coordinate in the Location panel. That needs no map and no data, and gives the screen one working, honest instrument.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):**

- Nothing on the page can be reached with the Tab key. All four controls are disabled, so they can't take focus.
- The one real capability, coordinate parsing, is locked behind the disabled search.
- There are no shortcuts, and no task can be completed in 60 seconds.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent):**

- **Reading order differs below 1024px.** The visual order is top bar, viewport, sidebar, details, but the DOM order is top bar, sidebar, main, details.
- **The disabled controls' reasons may go unannounced.** Disabled controls can't take focus, so their aria-describedby reasons can be missed in a screen reader's forms or focus mode.
- **A very small shape difference.** The filled-vs-hollow dot is only 6px across, so for low vision the badge's text label is what carries the meaning.
- **What passes:** the headings and landmarks are clean, and all text contrast is AA.

**Hackathon judge** (from PRODUCT.md: sees the demo, and needs the idea, the data sources and why sites rank):

- No lander, ranking or "why" anywhere on screen.
- No pointer to the sources, and no sign that 16 have been researched.
- The focal word on the screen is "Placeholder".
- The key honesty copy is 11px and faint.
- "CLPS" is never spelled out, and there's no non-endorsement line.

## Minor Observations

- **Convention ranges shown twice:** the convention ranges appear in both the fact list and the description text.
- **Uneven spacing:** the gap inside sections differs between the sidebar (--space-2) and the details panel (--space-3).
- **Placeholder fills the phone screen:** on phones, the 58vh viewport placeholder pushes all real content below the fold.
- **Accent does nothing:** accent-color on checkboxes that are always disabled has no visible effect.
- **Basemap row breaks the pattern:** it is styled as a control well but is plain prose with no status.
- **Single-use token:** --color-surface-raised is used once, in the vignette. A viewport-specific name would be clearer if it stays that way.
- **Lots of small caps:** with 11px uppercase section labels, subtitle and badges, much of the screen is in small caps.
