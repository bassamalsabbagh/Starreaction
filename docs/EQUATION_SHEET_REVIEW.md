## Numbers that disagree with the app

1. **Month length:** 29.53 days in the sheet; the app checks a 29.5-day range (a display range;
   positions are unaffected).
2. **Sunlight duration (M5):** 14-day window and 10-minute steps in the sheet; the app uses the
   29.5-day month and 1-hour steps.
3. **Earth elevation (§4.1):** the sub-point formula leaves out the Earth's parallax (up to about
   0.26°); the app includes it. The table values still agree to the sheet's precision.
4. **E_ref = 10°:** the demo missions' lowest Earth heights are 5°, 1° and 10° (DEMO values).
5. **Night length:** Miral's example uses 328 h (LuSEE-Night's planned night, SRC-046); the app
   measures the longest night at the place (for example 353 h at Demo Site Bravo this month).
6. **1361 W/m²:** "not yet source-verified" in the sheet; now SRC-044 (NASA Moon Fact Sheet).
7. **Weights:** the sheet notes another document gives the hazard a 0.25 weight. Moot here: the app
   uses no weights.
